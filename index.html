PickleMixRR

Pickleball • Mixer • Round Robin

The PickleMixRR app easily manages small group pickleball sessions with equitable matches. It can accomodate matches with varying numbers of male and female players, all for competitive fun!

For mixed groups, the app emphasizes playing games within each gender and across each gender whenever possible. For rankings, the female players are ranked against other females and the male players are ranked against other males. The event concludes with an optional Gold Medal Game with the top two players in each group in a mixed doubles matchup.

For gender or co-ed play, there is no distinction between male or female players. The event concludes with an optional Gold Medal Game with the top four players.
